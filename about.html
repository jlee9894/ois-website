Our Story

A Leading Provider of Moving & Office Services

Office and Industrial Specialists, a minority owned business, has become a leading provider of moving and office services. We take pride in offering the best service available.

Along with being a supplier of office furniture, we have added office supplies sales and delivery in order to serve the growing needs of our customers. We are dedicated to serving the needs of our customers each and every day.
