Our Services

Full-service office and industrial support — from planning through installation.

Office Relocation

Moving your business is more than trucks and boxes — it's keeping your people productive through the transition. OIS plans and executes office moves of any size with minimal disruption to daily operations.

  • Pre-move planning and scheduling
  • Careful handling of furniture, equipment, and files
  • Coordinated move-day logistics
  • Set-up support at your new location

Office Design / Space Planning

The OIS design team has the ability to assess the layout of your facility and provide options for the maximum utilization of space, while creating a welcoming atmosphere for your employees and customers. Our planning services cover office and warehouse spaces alike.

  • Facility layout assessment
  • Space utilization recommendations
  • Design options tailored to your workflow

Office Furniture Sales

As a supplier of office furniture, OIS helps outfit your space with quality pieces that fit your budget, brand, and layout — from individual workstations to full-office builds.

Office & Warehouse Supplies

Confidential Records Management & Storage

Secure, organized storage solutions for your business records, freeing up valuable office space while keeping your documents accessible when you need them. Our 100,000 sq. ft. facility handles both short- and long-term storage needs.

Office & Industrial Installation

From office furniture to industrial equipment, our full-time, in-house trained installation team uses top-of-the-line tools and trucks of every size to get jobs of any scale set up correctly and ready to use — done right the first time.
